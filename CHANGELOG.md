# Changelog

## 1.0.0 – 2026-11-05

First public release.

- **Brushes:** Draw convex brushes in the 3D viewport with grid snapping, then move, resize and rotate them.
- **Shapes:** Cylinders, polygons (also concave), stairs, pipes and walls, all adjustable after creation.
- **Textures:** Material browser, world-aligned UVs, painting and picking with Alt, face inspector and a graphical UV editor.
- **Editing:** Vertex, edge and face editing, clipping, subtract, Cube-Cut, hollow, merge, bevel and bridge.
- **Organization:** Groups, hide and isolate, and compounds that show split shapes as one object.
- **Output:** Merged meshes and collision saved with the scene, so levels run without the addon.
- **Interface:** English and German, with customizable shortcuts.
